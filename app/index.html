Portland, Maine

Portland synthetic population

A 3D map of about 68,700 synthetic people placed on Portland's 17,000 parcels, built from public Census and State of Maine data. You can attach a health measure, such as Alzheimer's, to every person and watch the map change as you adjust the model.

Open the map Code and method on GitHub

How to use it

  1. Zoom to change the level. City → 20 tracts → 61 block groups → 811 blocks → 17,019 parcels
  2. Click any area to see its households and people: age and sex, race, education, and how it compares with the block group, county and state.
  3. Use the left panel to colour the map by a variable, filter areas, search an address, or export CSV or GeoJSON.
  4. Try a data point. Under Data points, pick the Alzheimer's preset or click New data point, then drag the sliders:
    • Who is counted sets the ages and living situations included.
    • Baseline is the rate for a reference person.
    • Odds ratios raise or lower risk for each group; ×1 means no change.
    The map updates as you drag. Save as variable keeps the model for everyone using the site.

Reading the numbers

Expected cases
Each synthetic person's probability from the model, added up.
Rate
Expected cases divided by the people counted.
± margin
A 90% margin of error, as the Census uses. It covers uncertainty in the population, not in the model's odds ratios.
Confidence
high medium low How big the margin is compared with the estimate.
Small areas
Parcel and block totals are fairly firm. Who lives there is a likely mix, so read it as a probability.

Every value is a synthetic estimate of how people are likely distributed, not a record of real residents. Built from the ACS 2020–2024 (tables and PUMS microdata), the 2020 Census, and Maine parcel and E911 address data. The city total is 68,711 ± 506, against 68,854 in the ACS.
