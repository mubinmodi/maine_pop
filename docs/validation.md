# Validation report

Synthetic estimates for Portland, Maine, aggregated from parcels, compared with published Census figures. Generated by `synthpop validate`; do not edit by hand.

## Headline

- **City people**: 68,711 ± 506 synthetic vs 68,854 ± 52 ACS (90% margins; difference -0.2%)
- **City households**: 33,287 ± 555 synthetic vs 32,965 ± 785 ACS (90% margins; difference +1.0%)
- **City people 65+**: 11,482 ± 749 synthetic vs 11,616 ± 772 ACS (90% margins; difference -1.2%)
- **Block-group cells inside the ACS 90% margin**: 99.9% of 5,103
- **Held-out blocks, population share**: correlation 0.83 with 2020 Census (land-area baseline 0.68) across 162 blocks

## 1. Fit to published ACS, by level

Synthetic counts from the main run, summed up from parcels, against ACS 2020–2024 published estimates. RAE (relative allocation error, as in UrbanPop) is |synthetic − published| / published 90% MOE, so RAE ≤ 1 means the synthetic value is inside the published margin. Block groups are what the model fits to. Tracts and the city are independent ACS estimates, so they test whether block-group fits add up correctly. Tracts that extend beyond the study area are left out. Two expected gaps: housing units are 2020 Census block counts while ACS covers 2020–2024, and the study area is the 61 block groups at least half inside the city, which hold 93 fewer people than the city's own ACS total.

## 4. Uncertainty

Every estimate is the average of 21 placements of the main run, so a parcel shows its expected count (for example 0.94 households), not one random draw. Its standard error combines two sources. Survey uncertainty comes from 80 replicate runs (successive-difference formula): each uses one PUMS replicate weight set and shifts ACS targets within their margins, and they share the main run's placement random numbers. Placement uncertainty is the spread across the 21 placements. Confidence grades a value by its coefficient of variation: high ≤ 12%, medium ≤ 40%, low above. The grades below are for household counts (count confidence). The people 65+ column shows how much less certain composition is at small levels.

## 5. Fit and placement diagnostics

- Block-group fits converged: 100%; median iterations 11.
- Households placed on a parcel of their own structure type: 80.4%; nearest type: 14.4%; beyond parcel unit counts (flagged): 5.2%.
- Parcels with housing units: 16,955; given units only by fallback: 1.
- Group-quarters residents: 1,959 synthetic vs 1,959 ACS. The ACS figure is total population minus household population, so it carries the error of both.

## 6. Known limits

- Below the block, nothing can be checked against published data. Parcel values are placements, graded by their replicate spread, which is wide for small parcels by design.
- Unit counts come from E911 address points, not assessor records. Parcels without a usable address point get no housing unless their block has no other option.
- Seed households come from one PUMA (Portland, South Portland, Westbrook). Household types that are rare there are rare in every block group.
